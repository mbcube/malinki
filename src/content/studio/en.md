faq_q2_question: "What's in your studio?"
faq_q2_answer: 
  - "Our 1,000 sq ft studio, located in Rosemont, is designed to be functional, comfortable, and suitable for a variety of production types."
  - "It features a white cyclorama 15 feet wide, 8.6 feet deep and 11 feet high, steel pipes recessed in the ceiling for hanging equipment, and a HMU station with a makeup table, changing room, clothing rack and steamer."
  - "A loading dock and freight elevator make equipment access easy. The studio also includes a kitchen (fridge, microwave, air fryer, sink and dishes), great coffee, air conditioning, bathroom access in the corridor, and available parking ($)."
  - "You can build walls to create full room sets, such as a living room, office, or other setting."
faq_q3_question: What are the booking terms?
faq_q3_answer: 
  - Payment is required at the time of booking to confirm your reservation.
  - Any cancellation made less than 48 hours before the booking date is non-refundable.
faq_q4_question: What are the studio usage conditions?
faq_q4_answer: 
  - The studio must be returned to its original condition upon departure.
  - "Any material damage caused by the client or their party will result in repair or replacement fees. This includes, but is not limited to, damage to the cyclorama, furniture, and equipment."
  - "If the studio is not properly reset, cleaning fees of up to $150 may be charged depending on the condition of the studio."
  - "Please avoid glitter, feathers, confetti, powders, and other hard-to-clean materials. If in doubt, ask us beforehand."
  - The cyclorama surface must be kept clean. We ask that you wear shoe covers or clean indoor shoes when on the cyclo.
  - Please let us know in advance if you plan to bring a pet.
  - "Respect your neighbours: keep noise levels (music, voices) at a reasonable level to avoid disturbing surrounding tenants."
faq_q5_question: Is parking available?
faq_q5_answer: 
  - One parking spot is included.
  - Additional parking can be reserved for $25/day.
  - A loading dock and freight elevator are accessible for equipment transport.
faq_q6_question: What time slots are available?
faq_q6_answer:

  - It is also possible to book the studio for more specific time slots — simply contact us to arrange your reservation.
