The **Opéra de Montréal** renews its collaboration with **[Malinki](https://malinki.ca)** for a fifth consecutive season.

Led by **Pierre Vachon**, the web series capsules *PréOpéra Express* have become a must: they help audiences better grasp the history, themes, and musicality of the works on the bill before each performance.

Launched in 2020 to preserve these moments of cultural outreach in a virtual format, the series highlights Pierre Vachon’s expertise through dynamic visual content created by Malinki.

“Conceived as a friendly gateway into the world of opera, the *PréOpéra Express* series helps audiences discover the essentials of each work before they attend in the auditorium. Broadcast on social media and on Savoir media’s web platform, it also aims to spark curiosity among a new audience and make opera more accessible to everyone,” says the Opéra de Montréal.

Thanks to this collective effort, audiences enjoy an enriching, accessible, and vibrant experience that lets them step into the world of opera even before the curtain rises.
