L’**Opéra de Montréal** renews its collaboration with **[Malinki](https://malinki.ca)** for a fifth consecutive season.

Driven by **Pierre Vachon**, the web series capsules *PréOpéra Express* have become a must-see: they help the audience better understand the history, themes, and musicality of the works on the program before each performance.

Launched in 2020 in order to preserve these moments of cultural mediation in a virtual format, the series highlights Pierre Vachon’s expertise through dynamic visual content created by Malinki.

“Designed as a friendly gateway into the world of opera, the series *PréOpéra Express* helps audiences discover the essentials of each work before they arrive in the hall. Broadcast on social networks and on Savoir media’s web platform, it also aims to spark curiosity in a new audience and make opera more accessible to everyone,” says the Opéra de Montréal.

Thanks to this collective effort, the public enjoys an enriching, accessible, and vibrant experience that allows people to step into the world of opera even before the curtain rises.
