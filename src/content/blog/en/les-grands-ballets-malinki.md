[**The Canadian Grand Ballets**](https://grandsballets.com/fr/) turned to [**Malinki**](https://malinki.ca/) for the production of the new advertising campaign surrounding Songes d’une nuit d’été, choreographed by **Guillaume Côté**.  
  
The advertisement highlights the dancer **Maude Sabourin**, accompanied by **Kiara DeNae Felder**, as well as the corps de ballet of the Grand Ballets. The campaign translates the world halfway between dream and reality into images, in an approach where movement and a mysterious atmosphere take center stage.
