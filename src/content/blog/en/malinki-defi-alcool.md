The agency [**Nucleus**](https://nucleusstrategie.com/) trusted the production agency **[Malinki](https://malinki.ca/)** to handle the production and filming of the web video capsules for the [**28 Days Without Alcohol Challenge**](https://defi28jours.com/). Through these capsules, the goal is to break the taboo surrounding addictions that affect different age groups, genders, and social circles.

Malinki’s team produced a series of capsules highlighting the Challenge ambassadors and moving testimonials. The agency succeeded in meeting the challenge of telling more sensitive stories by designing a format that, despite the seriousness of the subject, remains accessible and enjoyable to watch. The humor of the ambassadors supports the playful side of the Challenge, while the testimonials add authentic depth. Despite these distinct approaches, the goal remains the same: to raise awareness of the disease.

“Through its expertise, Malinki managed to deliver capsules and testimonials full of authenticity and emotion while respecting people and their lived experience,” said **Ann-Julie Tremblay**, CEO of the [**Jean Lapointe Foundation**](https://fondationjeanlapointe.org/).

For a first collaboration between Nucleus and Malinki, the objectives have been met. “This first collaboration with Malinki succeeded in capturing the essence of the mission behind the 28 Days Without Alcohol Challenge. Together, we brought our vision of the capsules to life: authentic testimonies, delivered with sensitivity, that engage and directly contribute to the success of fundraising for the Jean Lapointe Foundation,” said **Clément Roy**, founder of Nucleus.

The 28 Days Without Alcohol Challenge raises awareness of the benefits of taking a break from consumption while supporting the Jean Lapointe Foundation in its prevention mission. Thanks to the collaboration between Nucleus and Malinki, these video capsules offer an authentic and inspiring look at the realities experienced around alcohol.
