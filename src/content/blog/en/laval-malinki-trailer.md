The [**City of Laval**](https://www.laval.ca/), in partnership with [**Tourisme Laval**](https://www.tourismelaval.com/), unveils a promotional trailer aimed at positioning the region as a top choice for filming TV series and international films. Produced by [**Malinki**](https://malinki.ca/), the video highlights the diversity of Laval filming locations through an approach that focuses on imagination rather than a simple inventory of sets.  
  
The trailer is aimed at production teams, and more particularly location researchers. In line with the slogan « Laval. An island of exceptions. », it illustrates the range of filming possibilities offered across the territory.  
  
The tone inspired by film narration turns each shot into a space where production teams can project their vision. It features excerpts from fictional films in order to demonstrate the narrative potential of the territory, archival footage, and previous productions shot in Laval. An immersive sound design supports this narration and sparks the imagination.  
  
With this outreach tool for the audiovisual industry, the City of Laval, Tourisme Laval and Malinki position the island as a versatile, inspiring and distinctive filming location!
