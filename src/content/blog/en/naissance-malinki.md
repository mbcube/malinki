Together, with more than 25 years of experience in the audiovisual field, **Andrew Tchernilevskii** and **Miroslav Dufresne** joined their talents to give birth to **[Malinki](https://malinki.ca/)**, an innovative video production agency. Since its launch in 2021, it has produced a wide range of visual projects. Today, Malinki officially unveils its [demo reel](https://vimeo.com/912326021?share=copy), showcasing several of its flagship projects.

![Malinki](https://assets.grenier.qc.ca/uploads/images/Logo_Malinki_2024-329522.jpg)

Andrew’s web content knowledge and Miroslav’s technical expertise make Malinki a go-to partner for producing commercials and branded content. Its client portfolio, including **Vidéotron**, **the Opéra de Montréal**, **Cook it**, **Fizz**, **St-Hubert**, **Grey Goose**, **Microsoft**, **TVA Sports**, and **AsterX**, demonstrates its know-how.

At Malinki, the focus is on a personalized client experience, aimed at making the content creation process enjoyable and transparent. By prioritizing close collaboration with its clients, the agency positions itself as a natural extension of their in-house content team. Its responsiveness is also praised by its partners, thanks to an internal structure that enables projects to be implemented quickly, from concept through post-production.

2024 marks a new era for Malinki, which plans to unveil an entirely new studio in the spring. To learn more about the agency, visit its [website](https://malinki.ca/).
